Nowsee
--:--

Nowsee draws whatever your Mac is playing, as it plays.

It taps the output mix, so it works with anything that makes sound — Spotify, a video call, a game. It lives in the menu bar: no dock icon, no window on launch, no account.

brew tap sergio-prog/tap brew trust --cask sergio-prog/tap/nowsee brew install --cask nowsee

Nowsee asks to record system audio. Nothing is written to disk, nothing leaves your Mac, and the microphone is never touched.
